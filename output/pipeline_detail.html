Step 2 · BERT Exploration
FrameBERT + FramingBERT
Two BERT classifiers explored the corpus. Neither produced a direct metaphor representation — but together they revealed two complementary angles.
FrameBERT — Semantic metaphors

Detects non-literal word use

Identifies words used figuratively. Captures lexical metaphor at the word level — but cannot tell us what conceptual role the government is assigned.

Limitation

Only flags surface figurative language. A government that "crushed" vs. one that "supported" are equally metaphorical — it cannot distinguish the roles.

FramingBERT (Entman) — Framing functions

Multilabel: 4 rhetorical functions

Define Problem · Diagnose Cause · Moral Judgment · Suggest Remedy. Shows what the article does rhetorically — not what the government is framed as.

Limitation

Structural label only. Knowing 67% of articles "suggest a remedy" doesn't tell us whether the government is the remedy or the problem.

Key insight — Two angles on metaphor

Semantic angle → Step 4

What source domain is the government mapped onto? → Open-ended noun metaphor labelling.

Functional angle → Step 3

What does the government do, and how is it evaluated? → Sentence-level extraction of Subject + Action + Evaluation.
